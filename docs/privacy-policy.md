# Privacy Policy: Lighthouse Fellowship app

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

This policy covers the **Lighthouse Fellowship** mobile app (Android and iOS), published for
Lighthouse Fellowship Napoli (ICF Napoli), Naples, Italy ("we", "the church").

## The short version

The app does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information. It has no
accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. You can use everything in it without
signing in.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves the following **only on your device**, so it works offline and remembers where you
were:

* the sermon notes you have opened (a cached copy);
* the last note you opened;
* your answers and scores in the fill-in-the-blank game.

This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage,
deletes it.

## What our server receives

To show the sermon notes, the app downloads them from the church's server. Like any connection
on the internet, each request carries your device's IP address. We use it only to protect the
server from abuse (limiting how many requests one address can make). It is kept in memory for a
few minutes, is not written to a database, and is not shared with anyone.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information about you. The app contains no third-party
advertising, analytics or tracking code.

## Children

The app contains church sermon notes and is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal
information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

A future version of the app may add an optional sign-in (by email code) to save game progress
across devices. Before that is released, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what is
collected, why, and how to delete it. Using the app without signing in will always remain
possible.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: **info@lighthousefellowshipnapoli.org**.
Under the GDPR you have the right to ask what data we hold about you and to have it deleted;
for this app, that is nothing beyond what is described above.
