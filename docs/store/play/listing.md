# Google Play listing

Copy these into Play Console → **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing**. Limits are
Google's.

## App name (max 30)

Lighthouse Fellowship

## Short description (max 80)

Sunday sermon notes from Lighthouse Fellowship, with a fill-in-the-blank game.

## Full description (max 4000)

Follow along with the Sunday sermon at Lighthouse Fellowship Napoli (ICF Napoli).

Every week's sermon notes are in the app, laid out like the paper notes handed out in church:
the scripture readings, the main points and the reflection questions. Turn the pages like a
book.

PLAY THE NOTES
Switch to Play mode and fill in the blanks yourself. Drag each word from the tray onto the right
blank, or tap a word and then a blank. When you finish, see your score, which answers were
right, and try again.

ALWAYS WITH YOU
• This Sunday's note opens straight away on the home screen.
• Browse every past sermon by month in the archive.
• Notes you have opened work offline.
• Your answers and best scores are saved on your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. The app does not collect your name or email.
